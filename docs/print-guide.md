# Print guide

A print shop makes a stiff card that lasts. The home sheet gets you a card today on your own printer.

## At a print shop

### What to hand over

The PDF for a print shop, from the Download menu. It has two pages, front and back, each the card's exact size. That's 85.60 × 53.98 mm for the wallet card and 105 × 74 mm for the pocket card. All text is outlined, so the shop needs no fonts.

### What to ask for

- Print at 100%, with scaling off. Fit to page shrinks the card.
- Print double sided, with the back the right way up when you turn the card over sideways, like a page.
- Use 250 gsm or heavier matte stock, and laminate it. Or ask for a PVC card the shop already cuts to bank card size.
- Round the corners to about 3 mm.

## At home

### What to print

The Print at home PDF, from the Download menu. It's one A4 or Letter page, whichever your country uses, with the front above the back. The wallet card comes two to a sheet, so you have a spare. The pocket card comes one to a sheet. The content fits inside both paper sizes, so the card comes out the right size on either.

### How to make the card

1. Print at 100% or Actual size. Turn off Fit to page and Shrink to fit.
2. Check the size. Lay a bank card on the printed wallet card, and they should match.
3. Cut along the corner marks, round the front and back together as one piece.
4. Fold along the line between them, where the short dashed marks are, so the back sits behind the front.
5. Glue the two halves together, or go straight to laminating. A laminating pouch keeps it flat and waterproof.

The back is printed upside down on the sheet on purpose. After the fold, it reads the right way up when you turn the card over sideways, like a page.

## Checking the print

Lay a bank card on top of the wallet card. They should be the same size. Text at the smallest size should still read at arm's length.

## PNG

The PNG carries its physical size in the file. Print software that respects it places the card at 85.60 × 53.98 mm. Software that ignores it assumes 96 dpi and prints the card several times too large, so check that the print dialog shows the right size.
